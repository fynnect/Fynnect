Regulatory Disclosures

Who we are

FYNNECT is an AMFI Registered Mutual Fund Distributor (ARN-350960). We help investors choose mutual fund schemes suited to their goals and risk profile, assist with executing transactions, and provide after-sales support.

FYNNECT is not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser. Any recommendation we make is limited to mutual fund schemes and is incidental to distribution, based on our assessment of your risk profile and the suitability of the scheme for your goal. We do not offer financial planning or advice on products other than mutual funds. For holistic financial planning, please consult a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser.

Tax filing support (ITR filing, capital gains computation and advance tax) is provided by a Chartered Accountant as a separate service. It is not part of our mutual fund distribution activity.

AMFI Registration Number (ARN): ARN-350960
Certification: NISM Series V-A (Mutual Fund Distributors)

You can verify the registration of any mutual fund distributor using the "Locate a Distributor" facility on the AMFI website.

How we are paid

  • We do not charge you a fee for mutual fund distribution services.
  • We earn trail commission from mutual fund houses (AMCs), directly or through our distribution platform, on investments made through us in the Regular plans of mutual fund schemes.
  • This commission is included in the scheme's Total Expense Ratio (TER). That is why the Regular plan of a scheme has a higher expense ratio than its Direct plan. You may invest in Direct plans on your own, without a distributor.
  • Commission rates differ across fund houses and schemes. On request, we will disclose the commission we receive on any scheme we recommend, and on competing schemes, so you can make an informed choice.
  • We do not rebate or pass back commission to investors, and we do not offer gifts or other inducements to invest.

Our commitments to you

As required under the AMFI Code of Conduct for Mutual Fund Distributors, we:

  • Treat your interest as paramount and recommend schemes only after assessing your risk profile and the suitability of the scheme for your goal.
  • Explain that mutual funds are not guaranteed or assured-return products and that the principal amount may be exposed to risk of loss.
  • Provide you with the scheme documents (SID, KIM and SAI) and highlight the key features, risks, costs and exit loads of any scheme we recommend.
  • Do not encourage churning or unnecessary switching of your investments.
  • Inform you in writing if a transaction you choose to make on your own ("execution only") appears unsuitable for you.
  • Disclose any conflict of interest that cannot be avoided.

Risk statement

Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully.

Past performance may or may not be sustained in future and is not a guarantee of future returns. Content on this website, including articles and guides, is for general education only and is not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any scheme.

Grievance redressal

  1. Contact us first. Email deepak@fynnect.com with your name, folio number (if any) and a description of the issue. We will work with you, and with the fund house where needed, to resolve it.
  2. Fund house or registrar. If your complaint relates to a scheme or transaction, you may also write directly to the concerned AMC or its registrar (CAMS or KFintech).
  3. SEBI SCORES. If the matter is not resolved satisfactorily, you may lodge a complaint on SEBI's SCORES portal: scores.sebi.gov.in.
  4. Online Dispute Resolution. Unresolved disputes may be taken to the securities market's Online Dispute Resolution portal: smartodr.in.
