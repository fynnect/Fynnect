Our Story

We've seen how
the industry works.
We chose differently.

Before FYNNECT, we spent years inside some of India's largest financial firms. We sat through client meetings, watched products being pushed for commissions, and saw portfolios built around targets instead of people. Families were often sold investments they didn't fully understand.

And every time someone asked, "Is this actually right for me?" — the honest answer rarely came first. That never felt right to us.

So we built FYNNECT around clarity and trust. We recommend investments only after understanding your goals and risk profile, we explain every recommendation in plain language, and we are upfront about how we are paid. No pressure. No unnecessary complexity. Just helping you invest towards the future you actually want to build.

Common Questions

Answers, before you ask.

What does FYNNECT do?

We help you invest for your goals — risk profiling, fund selection, SIPs, reviews and consolidation. Tax filing support is offered through a Chartered Accountant.

How does FYNNECT get paid?

No fee to you. We earn trail commission from fund houses on Regular plans — it's included in the scheme's expense ratio, so Regular plans cost more than Direct plans. Scheme-wise commission is disclosed on request. Full disclosures.

What happens in the first call?

A conversation about your goals and comfort with risk. No pressure, no jargon.

Do you work with NRIs?

Yes — NRE/NRO routing, KYC and FATCA, and fund houses that accept your country.

Can you help with taxes and ITR filing?

Yes — ITR filing, capital gains and advance tax, by a Chartered Accountant.
